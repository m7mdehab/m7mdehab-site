## Technical SEO

## Structured data
Homepage emits a `ProfilePage` whose `mainEntity` is a `Person` with truthful `sameAs`, `worksFor`, `alumniOf` and `knowsAbout` relationships. Structured claims must also appear visibly on the page.

## AI search
The strategy is **Discoverable → Understood → Cited → Recommended**.
- Google: prioritize core SEO, people-first non-commodity content, crawlability and page experience. `llms.txt` is not a Google ranking mechanism.
- ChatGPT Search: allow `OAI-SearchBot` and keep CDN/WAF rules from accidentally blocking verified crawler traffic.
- Anthropic: allow `Claude-SearchBot` and `Claude-User` for search/user retrieval; training crawler policy is a separate decision.
- Perplexity: allow `PerplexityBot` for indexing/search visibility.
- Avoid conflating search crawlers with model-training crawlers.

## Machine-readable outputs

These are consistency/accessibility surfaces, not guaranteed ranking factors.
